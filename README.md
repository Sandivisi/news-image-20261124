Jensen Huang's return as Nvidia CEO sends the stock up 31% and lifts the Nasdaq 6.5%, COP31 collapses in Antalya with no climate deal, NATO sends an AWACS aircraft to Poland after three Russian missiles land there, Ukraine sinks the Russian corvette Merkury in Benghazi, South Korea briefly orders Ulleung Island residents to shelter after an aircraft enters its airspace, and a candy-factory fire in Cikande, Indonesia kills 24 with dozens still missing.
